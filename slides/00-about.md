# Claude for Business Professionals

Data, Reporting & Presentations

Elephant Scale

---

## What This Is

Four short sessions. By the end, you can point Claude at your own spreadsheet, your own
report, and your own deck — and get real work done, safely.

* No coding. No math. A browser and a Claude account.
* Half of every session is you, working in Claude.
* We build one thing across all four: data → findings → report → presentation.

Notes:

Set the tone: this is a working session, not a lecture about AI. Ask who has "tried it once
and wasn't impressed" — those are the people this course is for.

---

## How the Four Sessions Fit

1. **Getting productive** — how to talk to Claude so it actually helps
2. **Data analysis** — ask your spreadsheet questions, in plain English
3. **Reporting** — turn the numbers into writing someone will read
4. **Presentations** — turn the report into a deck, and make the skill stick

Notes:

Each session is ~60 minutes and ends in a hands-on lab. The labs chain — what you analyze in
Session 2 becomes the report in Session 3 and the deck in Session 4. Keep your work.

---

## What You'll Walk Out With

* An analysis of a real business dataset — with the error in it caught
* A polished one-page report, retargeted for two different readers
* A draft presentation with speaker notes
* A **personal prompt library** — the prompts that did the work, ready to reuse

Notes:

Emphasize the last one. The deliverable that matters most isn't any single document — it's
the reusable prompts they take back to their desk.

---

## Two Ground Rules

**1. Everything we touch in class is fictional.** Nothing from your own organization until
you're back at your desk and following your own policy. Session 1 covers exactly why.

**2. The words can be Claude's. The claims have to be yours.** You verify; you don't trust.
We'll practice that every session.

Notes:

These two rules run through the whole course. Say them now, and call back to them often.
